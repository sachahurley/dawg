# Example: one-line risk ping to PM

**Audience:** PM  
**Channel:** Slack  
**When to use:** Scope or dependency shifted; you need a fast read without a full deck.

---

**Heads-up — [Feature]:** [One sentence — e.g. “DS migration for checkout touches 3 legacy widgets; I’m slicing by step 1 this week, steps 2–3 next — no user-facing change until step 3 unless we pull forward.”]  
**Decision needed by [date] (if any):** [e.g. “Whether to slip polish ticket X to keep migration in this sprint.”]  
**Default if I don’t hear back:** [e.g. “I’ll keep the slice plan and move polish to next sprint.”]
