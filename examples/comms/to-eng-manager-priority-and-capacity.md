# Example: engineering manager (priority + capacity)

**Audience:** Engineering manager / EM  
**Channel:** Slack or 1:1 doc  
**When to use:** Two streams compete for the same people; you need sequencing without dictating implementation.

---

**Context:** [Feature A] and [Feature B] both touch the design system this sprint. From a **product/design system** perspective, **[A | B]** reduces duplicate patterns and unblocks **[downstream work]** first.

**Ask:** Can we sequence so **[chosen item]** lands in **[week/sprint]**? I’m happy to shrink scope on the other stream to a **design-complete** slice only if that helps planning.

**Non-goals:** I’m not asking to change estimates on your side — only to align **order** so we don’t migrate half a flow twice.

**If helpful:** I can join your next planning slice for 10m with a one-screen scope sketch.

Thanks.
